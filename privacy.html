1. Information we collect

When you submit a contact inquiry or quote request, we may collect your name, email address, company, phone number if supplied, service interests, project budget or timeline if supplied, and the message you choose to send.

2. How we use information

We use inquiry information to respond to requests, assess project requirements, communicate with you and operate, protect and improve this website. Do not send passwords, payment card information or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

3. Storage and service providers

Inquiry records are stored in the website's application database. If email notifications are configured, relevant inquiry details may also be sent through the configured SMTP provider. Hosting, email and infrastructure providers may process information to provide their services.

4. Retention and security

We retain inquiry information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, subject to legal obligations and operational needs. Reasonable safeguards should be maintained, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Cookies and sessions

The admin area uses a session cookie for authentication. The public website does not intentionally use advertising cookies in this starter implementation. Hosting or third-party services may have their own practices.

6. Your choices

You may contact us to ask about the personal information you have submitted or request correction or deletion, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping requirements.

7. Children

This website is intended for business inquiries and is not directed at children.
